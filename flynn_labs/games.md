# Games

Ever since I was young I've had a love for video games. From running across the street to a friends house to play Megaman on their SNES or saving up to by an Xbox to finally be able to play Halo at home - video games were a major part of growing up for me.

No one ever told me growing up, though, that there was actually people out there that their entire jobs were to design, build and create these games.

Fast-forward to today, where software is a bit easier to understand for me and the ecosystem of resources exists for almost anyone to create a game - I'd like to try my hand at that.

We'll see how far I get or if this idea will crash and burn like many of my others...but at least I'll learn something along the way.

Keep moving forward. :rocket:
